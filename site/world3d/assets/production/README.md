# Sanctum & Shadow production visuals

These GLB files are generated by `tools/blender/build_visual_library.py` and are runtime assets for the game.

- Buildings, interiors, vegetation, props, weapons, materials, and animation clips are original project-generated work built from Blender primitives.
- Character topology and the base armature derive from the CC0 Quaternius animated character documented in `site/prototype/ASSET-LICENSE.md`.
- The editable source library is `tools/blender/sanctum-visual-library.blend` and is kept outside this browser-served directory.

Regenerate the complete package with Blender 5.2 or later using the command in `tools/blender/README.md`.

## Third-party models

- `characters/heroes/fallen_paladin.glb` (player paladin hero): "Fallen Paladin in Corrupted Black Plate Armor" by Pigcraft (https://sketchfab.com/s8819296), source https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fallen-paladin-in-corrupted-black-plate-armor-df3056caff7e4f079bd4eec0fa9bcd54, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: rigged to the Sanctum & Shadow skeleton, sword separated, textures re-encoded and optimised for this game. Credited on the splash screen (`site/index.html`) and in the GLB's `asset.copyright`. It is intentionally not listed in `manifest.json`; `tests/world3d-hero.test.js` covers it.
  - Rebuild: `tools/hero/build-hero.sh copy <out-dir>` (Blender bind/export, clip transplant, `tools/hero/cape-drape.mjs` cape corrective, compression, `tools/hero/verify-hero.cjs` gates, `tools/hero/sword-sockets.py` socket check), then copy `<out-dir>/fallen_paladin.glb` here. The sword sockets live in `site/world3d/class-equipment.js` (`FALLEN_LONGSWORD_SOCKETS`): held in the right gauntlet with the crossguard against the index finger, stowed on the left hip at belt height.
  - Cape: one skinned sheet on the 49-joint contract plus one morph target, `FP_CapeDrape`, computed in the Death end pose; `character-actor.js` drives it only during Death (15-65 % of the clip) so the cape settles onto the falling body instead of standing over it as a dome.
  - Known, documented deviation (awaiting the owner's decision): in deep spine bends the cape still stretches more than the human.glb body on its longest edges (Attack_Heavy, a warrior clip; Hit; Attack_Smite by 1 mm; Death 111 vs 97 mm after the drape). Not visible as tearing at the gameplay camera. Further fix if wanted: BUILD-SPEC v1.1 cape bones or more per-clip correctives.
